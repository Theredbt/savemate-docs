The Vidow cast player lets you send any detected video stream to a Chromecast or AirPlay device on your network. Detect a video with the Vidow extension, click Cast, and it plays on your TV.

## How to cast a video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Detect a video with the Vidow extension">
    Navigate to a page with video content. The Vidow icon lights up when a stream is detected. Install the extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hkdmdpdhfaamhgaojpelccmeehpfljgf) if you haven't already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cast">
    Open the Vidow extension popup and click **Cast** on the detected stream.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your device">
    The cast player opens and shows Chromecast and AirPlay devices on your local network. Select the device you want to cast to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Video plays on TV">
    The stream begins playing on your selected device. Use the cast player controls to manage playback from your browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cast player features

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Device discovery** | Finds Chromecast and AirPlay devices on your local network |
| **Quality selection** | Choose from available quality levels for the stream |
| **Playback controls** | Play, pause, seek, and volume — controlled from your browser |
| **Thumbnail preview** | See a preview of the stream before and during casting |
| **Session tracking** | Each session gets a unique identifier; sessions expire after 2 hours of inactivity |

## Availability

The cast player is available on both Free and Professional plans. No account is required — anonymous users can cast too.

Free users may have session limits depending on current plan thresholds. Professional users have no session restrictions.

## Requirements

- A Chromecast, Chromecast with Google TV, or AirPlay-compatible device
- The cast device must be on the same Wi-Fi network as your computer
- A Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, or Brave)
- The [Vidow browser extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hkdmdpdhfaamhgaojpelccmeehpfljgf) for stream detection
