Dropbox authorization goes through OAuth 2.0. Vidow never handles your Dropbox password — you log in through Dropbox's own interface and grant access from there.

## Prerequisites

- A Vidow account on the [Professional plan](/billing/plans)
- A Dropbox account with storage available for video files

## Connect Dropbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cloud Sync">
    Go to [vidow.io/cloud-sync](https://vidow.io/cloud-sync) and sign in to your Vidow account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect on Dropbox">
    Find the Dropbox card and click **Connect**. You'll be taken to Dropbox's authorization page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Dropbox">
    Enter your Dropbox credentials if you aren't already logged in. You can also sign in with Google or Apple if your Dropbox account uses those.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Dropbox asks you to confirm that Vidow can write files to your account. Vidow operates within a dedicated folder only — nothing outside that folder is accessible.

    Click **Allow** to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connection confirmed">
    Dropbox redirects you back to Vidow. The Dropbox card updates to show a **Connected** status, and you're ready to sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How uploads work

Vidow uses the Dropbox upload API with chunked transfers for larger files:

- Files that fit within a single request are sent in one shot
- Larger files are broken into 8MB chunks and uploaded sequentially
- Progress is tracked per chunk, so the Cloud Sync page reflects how far along a transfer is
- Interrupted uploads do not need to restart from zero — Dropbox tracks which chunks were received

## Storage location

Vidow creates a dedicated folder inside your Dropbox. Files saved through Vidow go there and sync automatically to any device where you have Dropbox installed. You can access, move, or share them the same way you would with any other Dropbox file.

## Managing the connection

### Connection health

The Cloud Sync page shows the current state of your Dropbox token:

- **Healthy** — The token is valid and uploads are working
- **Broken** — The token expired or was otherwise invalidated. Click **Reconnect** to re-authorize
- **Revoked** — You removed Vidow's access from Dropbox. Use **Reconnect** to restore it

### Disconnect

To remove the integration from Vidow:

1. Go to [vidow.io/cloud-sync](https://vidow.io/cloud-sync)
2. Click disconnect on the Dropbox card

Your files in Dropbox are not affected — only the Vidow connection is removed.

To also revoke access from Dropbox's side, go to [dropbox.com/account/connected_apps](https://www.dropbox.com/account/connected_apps) and remove Vidow from the list.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Upload stuck or very slow">
    Dropbox imposes API rate limits on third-party apps. If you're syncing many videos in rapid succession, some transfers may pause while Vidow waits for the rate limit window to reset. Give it a few minutes, then check the Cloud Sync page to see if the upload resumes on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Storage quota exceeded">
    Review your available space at [dropbox.com/account](https://www.dropbox.com/account). Free Dropbox accounts come with 2GB of storage, which can fill up quickly with video files. Upgrade your Dropbox plan or switch to a provider with more capacity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connection shows as broken">
    A broken connection usually means Dropbox invalidated the OAuth token — this can happen if you changed your Dropbox password or removed Vidow's permissions. Click **Reconnect** on the Cloud Sync page to complete a fresh authorization.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
