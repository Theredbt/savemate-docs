The single most common question new users ask is some version of: "Can Video Downloader Plus download YouTube videos?" The answer is no — and the reason has nothing to do with a technical gap. No Chrome extension can do this, by design.

## Can Video Downloader Plus download YouTube videos?

No. Video Downloader Plus does not download YouTube videos, and neither does any other Chrome extension currently listed on the Chrome Web Store.

This is not a limitation of Video Downloader Plus specifically. It is a hard constraint imposed at the platform level, by both Google and YouTube.

## Chrome Web Store policy

Google's [Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies) include an explicit prohibition on extensions that enable downloading content from YouTube. The rule applies universally — no extension is exempt.

An extension that offers YouTube downloading will face one or more of the following outcomes:

1. **Rejected** — Google's review team identifies the capability during submission and rejects the extension before it reaches users
2. **Removed** — If the functionality slips through initial review, it is flagged and pulled from the store once discovered
3. **Banned** — The developer account associated with the extension may be permanently disabled

Video Downloader Plus remains on the Chrome Web Store because it follows these policies. That means it is subject to Google's full security review process, receives ongoing updates, and operates transparently.

## YouTube Terms of Service

YouTube's own [Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms) prohibit users from downloading content unless YouTube itself provides a download button or link for that specific video. YouTube Premium offers an official offline download feature within the YouTube app, which is the only sanctioned method for saving YouTube content locally.

## About extensions that claim otherwise

Occasionally, an extension appears on lesser-reviewed distribution channels claiming it can download YouTube videos. These extensions typically:

- Operate outside of Chrome Web Store review, which means no Google security audit
- Get removed from the store quickly when they appear, leaving users with an unmaintained extension
- Have been associated with ad injection, data harvesting, and other malicious behavior

<Warning>Exercise real caution with any extension or website that advertises YouTube downloading. If it was pulled from the Chrome Web Store, that removal happened for a reason. Extensions distributed outside the store have no independent verification and represent a genuine security risk.</Warning>

Video Downloader Plus works with an enormous range of video content on the web — specifically, anything delivered through standard streaming formats. YouTube is one of the few platforms that wraps its streams in proprietary protection specifically to prevent this. The rest of the web largely does not.

## YouTube alternatives for offline access

If you need offline access to YouTube content specifically, these are the legitimate paths:

### YouTube Premium

YouTube's official subscription tier includes a download feature built into the YouTube mobile and desktop apps. Downloads are locked to the app and expire after 30 days without an internet reconnect. Files cannot be transferred to other devices or media players.

### Built-in download buttons

Some YouTube videos display a download button below the player. This feature is provided by YouTube itself, typically for select creators or content categories. It is available to Premium subscribers and occasionally to non-subscribers for specific content.

### Creator-provided alternatives

Many creators publish their content on multiple platforms or share direct download links in video descriptions. If the content exists elsewhere — on Vimeo, a personal site, or another platform — Video Downloader Plus may be able to detect and download it from there.

Video Downloader Plus is built around long-term reliability over short-term workarounds. Chasing YouTube compatibility would mean operating outside the Chrome Web Store's policies, which would lead to removal and leave users running an unsupported extension.

- **Broad compatibility** — Thousands of sites across news, education, social media, sports, and corporate video all use standard streaming formats
