The Vidow library gives you a place to store references to videos you've found while browsing. Instead of bookmarking a URL and hoping the video is still there when you return, you save the stream directly — with its thumbnail, title, and source page recorded at the moment of detection.

## Saving videos

When Vidow detects a stream, click **Save** in the popup to add it to your library. For each saved video, Vidow stores:

- **Title** — The video title extracted from the page or stream metadata
- **Thumbnail** — A preview image captured at the time of saving
- **Source link** — The original page URL so you can return to where the video lives
- **Hash** — A content fingerprint used to deduplicate saves, so the same video doesn't appear twice even if saved from different sessions

## Library limits

| Plan | Saved videos |
|------|-------------|
| **Free** | Up to 10 videos |
| **Professional** | Unlimited |

<Note>
  When a Free account reaches 10 saved videos, saving additional entries requires removing existing ones or [upgrading to Professional](/billing/plans).
</Note>

## Accessing your library

Your saved videos are at [vidow.io/videos](https://vidow.io/videos). Sign in to view your collection. The library page includes:

- **Grid view** — Thumbnail-based browsing so you can visually scan your collection
- **Search** — Filter videos by title to find a specific one quickly
- **Sort** — Reorder the grid by date saved, title, or other available fields
- **Pagination** — Large libraries load in pages to keep the interface responsive
- **Direct links** — Each entry links back to the original source page

## Dashboard overview

The [Vidow dashboard](https://vidow.io/dashboard) shows a summary of your activity across the extension:

- **Total saved videos** — Your running count alongside a weekly trend indicator
- **Recent saves** — The most recently added entries to your library
- **Recent syncs** — Latest uploads to connected cloud storage providers
- **Plan usage** — Visual indicators for download and storage quotas on the Free plan
