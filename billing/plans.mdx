## Free plan

The free tier is a real plan, not a crippled trial. You get:

- **720p and SD downloads** with no daily cap
- **1080p downloads** at up to 3 per day
- **10-video library** to save content you want to keep
- **Chromecast** support so you can play on your TV

If you stay under 3 HD downloads a day and do not need cloud sync or format conversion, the free plan handles it without any payment.

## Professional plan

Professional removes every cap and unlocks every tool in the video downloader pro chrome extension:

- **Unlimited downloads** at any quality — 1080p, 4K, 8K, 60fps — with no daily ceiling
- **Unlimited library** so your saved video collection never hits a wall
- **Cloud sync** to push downloads directly to Google Drive, Dropbox, AWS S3, or Cloudflare R2
- **Format conversion** from HLS streams to MP4, MKV, MOV, or WebM
- **Audio extraction** to pull the audio track as MP3 or AAC
- **Priority support** with faster response times

The video downloader professional chrome extension also exposes advanced stream controls and higher-fidelity quality selection that are only active on Professional accounts.
