Everything subscription-related lives in one place: [vidow.io/billing](https://vidow.io/billing). You do not need to contact support to change your plan, swap a card, or grab a past invoice.

## Billing dashboard

When you open the billing page, you will see:

- **Current plan** — the plan name and its current status (Active, On Grace Period, Past Due)
- **Next billing date** — the exact date your subscription renews and charges your card
- **Payment methods** — saved cards with a clear indicator of which one is set as default
- **Invoices** — a full record of every charge with PDF download links

## Change your plan

### Upgrade to Professional

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the billing dashboard">
    Go to [vidow.io/billing](https://vidow.io/billing) and click **Upgrade**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a billing interval">
    Choose Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime. Current pricing for each option is shown inline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Complete checkout through the Stripe payment form. Your upgrade is active the moment the charge goes through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Switch between monthly and yearly

To move between billing intervals, open your billing dashboard and select the new interval. The change applies at the start of your next billing period. Any balance from the current period is credited proportionally so you are not charged for time you have already paid for.

### Cancel your subscription

To cancel, open the billing dashboard and click **Cancel subscription**. Cancellation does not cut off access immediately — your Professional features stay active through the end of the period you paid for. After that date, the account moves to the free plan automatically.

<Tip>
  Changed your mind before the period ends? You can resume a cancelled subscription from the billing dashboard at any point before the expiry date. Your access continues without interruption and no new billing cycle starts until the original one would have.
</Tip>

## Payment methods

### Add a card

Open the billing dashboard and click **Add payment method**. The Stripe secure form handles card entry — Vidow never sees or stores raw card numbers. Once added, the card is saved and available for future charges.

### Set a default card

If you have more than one card on file, click the card you want to use going forward. All upcoming subscription renewals and charges will route to the default card.

### Remove a card

Any card that is not currently set as the default can be removed. Click the remove button next to it. You cannot remove the active default card while a subscription is running — set a different card as default first.

## Invoices

Every charge generates an invoice in your billing dashboard. Each entry shows:

- Invoice number and issue date
- Plan name and the billing period it covers
- Total amount charged
- Payment method used for that charge
- A link to download the PDF receipt

Invoices are available immediately after each successful charge and are kept indefinitely in your account.

## Coupons and discounts

Coupon codes can be applied in two ways: during checkout before you confirm the first charge, or from the billing dashboard under **Apply coupon** after you are already subscribed. Once a coupon is active, the discount is shown on your plan summary so you always know the adjusted amount before it charges.
