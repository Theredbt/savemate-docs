## Stream not detected

**The Video Downloader Plus icon stays inactive on a page that has video.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the stream format">
    Video Downloader Plus detects HLS (m3u8), DASH (mpd), MP4, and WebM streams. Sites that serve video through proprietary DRM systems typically cannot be detected regardless of settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    Detection runs at page load. A full refresh gives Video Downloader Plus another opportunity to find the stream before any content is cached by the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play the video first">
    Many platforms delay loading the stream URL until you press play. Start the video, wait a few seconds, and check whether the Video Downloader Plus icon activates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the extension is enabled">
    Right-click the Video Downloader Plus icon in your toolbar and open **Manage extension**. Verify the toggle is set to **On** for the site you are visiting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download fails or is incomplete

**A download starts but stops early, or the output file is corrupted.**

- **Check your connection** — HLS downloads fetch many small segments in sequence. A dropped connection can interrupt this process.
- **Switch to a lower quality** — Smaller files place less strain on slow or unstable connections and are more likely to complete in one pass.
- **Retry the download** — Video Downloader Plus supports resuming interrupted HLS downloads from where they stopped. Start the download again rather than restarting from the beginning.
- **Clear browser cache** — Stale manifest data can cause the extension to request segments that no longer exist. Clear your cache, refresh the page, and try again.
- **Verify available disk space** — Make sure the drive you are saving to has enough free space to hold the full video file.

## Extension not visible in toolbar

**You installed Video Downloader Plus but cannot find the icon in your browser.**

The extension is installed but may not be pinned yet:

1. Click the **puzzle piece icon** in the upper-right corner of your browser window
2. Locate Video Downloader Plus in the list of installed extensions
3. Click the **pin icon** to keep it permanently visible in your toolbar

For detailed screenshots, see the [Pin to Toolbar guide](/extension/pinning).

## Cloud sync issues

**Uploads to cloud storage fail or do not start.**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Google Drive / Dropbox">
    - **Re-authorize the connection** — OAuth tokens expire over time. Go to your Cloud Sync settings, disconnect the provider, and reconnect to generate a fresh token.
    - **Review granted permissions** — During the OAuth flow, Video Downloader Plus requires specific access scopes. If you declined any permissions, the connection may be partially authorized. Disconnect and reconnect, granting all requested permissions.
    - **Check storage quota** — Your Google Drive or Dropbox account may be full. Free up space before retrying the upload.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AWS S3 / Cloudflare R2">
    - **Verify your credentials** — Confirm that the Access Key ID and Secret Access Key entered in Video Downloader Plus settings are current and correct.
    - **Check bucket permissions** — The IAM user or policy attached to your credentials must include `s3:PutObject` and `s3:CreateMultipartUpload` at minimum.
    - **Confirm the region** — An incorrect region setting will cause all requests to fail. Match the region in Video Downloader Plus settings to the region shown in your bucket configuration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account issues

### Cannot verify email

If the verification email did not arrive:

1. Check your spam and junk folders — transactional email can be filtered automatically
2. Go to [vidow.io/email/verify](https://vidow.io/email/verify) and click **Resend verification email**
3. If you use a work or school address, ask your IT team whether outbound email from external domains is filtered

### Forgot password

1. Open [vidow.io/forgot-password](https://vidow.io/forgot-password)
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Check your inbox for the reset link and follow the instructions to set a new password

### Locked out after 2FA

If you have lost access to your authenticator app:

1. Use one of the recovery codes you saved when you set up 2FA to log in
2. Once inside your account, disable 2FA and re-enable it with a working device

If you no longer have your recovery codes either, contact support for manual account recovery.

## Download limit reached

**Video Downloader Plus shows a message saying you have hit your daily limit.**

Free accounts include 2 video downloads per day, at any resolution (up to 4K/8K).

- **The limit resets at midnight** — Wait until the following day and your allocation refreshes automatically.
- **The limit follows your account, not your device** — Switching browsers or machines will not bypass the cap if you are logged in.
- **Remove the cap permanently** — [Professional plans](/billing/plans) include unlimited downloads with no daily limit.

<Tip>If you regularly need more than 2 downloads per day, [Lifetime Access](/billing/lifetime) is a one-time payment that removes the cap and all other Professional restrictions indefinitely.</Tip>

## Cloud upload stuck

**An upload shows as in progress but the progress bar is not moving.**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Check your connection health">
    Open [vidow.io/cloud-sync](https://vidow.io/cloud-sync) and look at the status indicator on your provider card:

    - **Healthy** (green) — The connection is authorized and working. The stall is likely network-related.
    - **Broken** (yellow) — Your OAuth token expired. Click **Reconnect** to re-authorize without losing your settings.
    - **Revoked** (red) — Access was removed from the provider side. You will need to reconnect and re-authorize from scratch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pause and resume the upload">
    For S3 and R2 multipart uploads, pausing and resuming creates a fresh connection while keeping already-uploaded parts intact. No data is re-uploaded from the beginning.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check your storage quota">
    A full cloud storage account will silently reject new uploads. Confirm your provider account has enough available space before retrying. Google Drive shares its quota across Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Retry a failed upload">
    Failed uploads appear in your upload history on the Cloud Sync page. Click the retry button next to any failed entry to attempt the upload again from where it stopped.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payment issues

**A payment was declined or your subscription has not activated.**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Card declined">
    1. Go to [vidow.io/billing](https://vidow.io/billing) and update your payment method
    2. Retry the payment from your billing dashboard

    Common reasons for a decline: insufficient funds, an expired card, or automatic fraud protection from your bank. Contact your bank directly if the card should be working.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invoice payment failed">
    Failed invoices can be retried manually from your billing dashboard. Video Downloader Plus also schedules automatic retry attempts after a short delay, so the payment may succeed on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Subscription not activating after payment">
    Subscription activation relies on a payment confirmation from Stripe. Processing usually takes under a minute, but can occasionally run longer. If your Professional features have not appeared within 5 minutes of payment:

    1. Refresh the page
    2. Log out and log back in
    3. Contact support if the account still shows as free
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Extension does not detect videos on single-page apps

**Video Downloader Plus misses videos when you navigate between pages without a full reload.**

Video Downloader Plus includes a built-in SPA observer that watches for navigation changes on React, Vue, Angular, and similar frameworks. If detection is still not triggering:

1. **Play the video** — Many SPAs do not fetch the stream URL until the player is actually started
2. **Wait a few seconds** — Navigation events fire asynchronously, so detection can lag a few seconds behind the route change
3. **Force a full page refresh** — Press F5 or Ctrl+R to reload the page entirely, which restarts the detection process from scratch
4. **Check your blocked sites list** — Confirm the domain is not excluded in your [extension settings](/extension/settings#website-controls)
