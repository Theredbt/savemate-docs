## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Video Downloader Plus free?">
    Yes. The free plan gives you unlimited 720p downloads, 3 HD downloads per day, Chromecast streaming, and a library that holds up to 10 saved videos. No credit card required to get started.

    Professional unlocks unlimited 4K and 8K downloads, cloud sync to Google Drive, Dropbox, S3, and R2, format conversion tools, and an unlimited video library.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Video Downloader Plus work on Mac and Windows?">
    Yes. Video Downloader Plus runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux. Any desktop machine running Chrome, Edge, or Brave is supported. There is no separate desktop app to install — the browser extension is all you need.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is it legal and safe?">
    Video Downloader Plus is built for personal archiving and fair use. The extension is listed on the Chrome Web Store and goes through Google's review process before each release.

    Video Downloader Plus processes video detection locally in your browser. Some data is sent to Video Downloader Plus servers for features like fallback detection and library sync — see our [privacy page](/privacy) for complete details.

    Always check the terms of service for the sites you visit and respect the rights of content creators.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Video Downloader Plus work on mobile?">
    No. Browser extensions are a desktop-only feature. Video Downloader Plus cannot be installed on mobile browsers, tablets, or mobile operating systems. Use it on any desktop running Chrome, Edge, or Brave.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Downloads

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I download a video?">
    1. Install Video Downloader Plus from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hkdmdpdhfaamhgaojpelccmeehpfljgf) (Edge users can also use the [Edge Add-ons store](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/video-downloader-plus/cnbnfjhmdaflilgadocmfindhmcfpfnc))
    2. Go to any page that has video content
    3. Wait for the Video Downloader Plus icon to activate in your toolbar
    4. Click the icon, pick a quality, and start the download

    For a full walkthrough, see the [Quick Start guide](/quickstart).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What video sites are supported?">
    Video Downloader Plus works on any site that delivers video over HLS (m3u8), DASH (mpd), MP4, or WebM. Detection is format-based, not site-specific, so it covers a broad range of streaming and video hosting platforms without needing per-site updates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Video Downloader Plus work with YouTube?">
    No. Chrome Web Store policies prohibit extensions from downloading YouTube content, and Video Downloader Plus complies with those policies to stay listed on the store. This applies to all Chrome extensions, not just Video Downloader Plus.

    Video Downloader Plus works across thousands of other sites that use standard streaming formats. Read our [full guide on YouTube and Chrome extensions](/guides/youtube-and-chrome-extensions) for context and alternatives.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if a download fails?">
    Video Downloader Plus has built-in retry logic for unstable connections. HLS downloads can resume from the point they stopped rather than restarting from the beginning. If a download still fails:

    1. Refresh the page and let Video Downloader Plus re-detect the stream
    2. Try a lower quality level — smaller segments are more tolerant of slow connections
    3. Check your internet connection
    4. Contact support if the problem keeps happening
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why am I limited to 720p?">
    Free accounts can download at 720p and below without any cap. Downloads at 1080p and above count against the 3 HD downloads per day limit on the free plan. [Upgrade to Professional](/billing/plans) to remove that restriction entirely.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Extension

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which browsers are supported?">
    Video Downloader Plus supports Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Brave. Firefox support is planned for a future release. The extension works on all three platforms — macOS, Windows, and Linux.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I update the extension?">
    Chrome, Edge, and Brave handle extension updates automatically in the background. You do not need to take any action to stay on the latest version.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the extension safe?">
    Yes. Video Downloader Plus is listed on the Chrome Web Store and passes Google's security review with each update. The extension requests only the permissions needed for video detection. See our [privacy page](/privacy) for what data is collected and why.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Lifetime Access work?">
    Lifetime Access is a single one-time payment that grants permanent access to all Professional features. There are no renewal charges and the access does not expire. Future features added to the Professional plan are included. Learn more on the [Lifetime Access page](/billing/lifetime).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
    Yes, at any time. Go to [vidow.io/billing](https://vidow.io/billing) and cancel from your billing dashboard. Your Professional access continues through the end of the current billing period, then the account moves to the free plan automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Video Downloader Plus accepts all major credit and debit cards — Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and others — processed securely through Stripe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
