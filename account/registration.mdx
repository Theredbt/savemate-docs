An account is not required to start using Video Downloader Plus. You can detect streams and run basic downloads the moment the extension is installed. An account becomes necessary when you want to store videos in your cloud library, sync across devices, or manage a subscription.

## Create an account

### Email registration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the registration page">
    Open [vidow.io/register](https://vidow.io/register) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter your name, email address, and a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    A confirmation link will be sent to the address you provided. Click it to activate your account. Authenticated features are not available until verification is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Google Sign-In

If you prefer not to manage a separate password, you can register through Google instead:

1. Click **Sign in with Google** on the registration or login page
2. Choose the Google account you want to use and grant Video Downloader Plus access
3. Your account is created immediately — no email verification step required

<Tip>
  Google Sign-In is the fastest way to get started. Your email is pre-verified through Google, so you gain full access right away.
</Tip>

## Sign in

Go to [vidow.io/login](https://vidow.io/login) and enter your email and password. If you registered through Google, use the **Sign in with Google** button instead.

If [two-factor authentication](/account/security) is active on your account, you will be prompted for a verification code after your password is accepted.

## Password reset

Forgot your password? Here is how to recover access:

1. Click **Forgot your password?** on the login page
2. Type in your account email address
3. Open the reset email that arrives in your inbox
4. Follow the link and choose a new password

## What does an account unlock?

| Feature | Without account | With account |
|---|---|---|
| Stream detection | Yes | Yes |
| Basic downloads | Yes | Yes |
| Chromecast | Yes | Yes |
| Cloud library | — | Yes (10 free, unlimited with Pro) |
| Cloud sync | — | Yes (Professional) |
| Billing management | — | Yes |
| Download history | — | Yes |

## Delete your account

Account deletion is available at [vidow.io/settings/profile](https://vidow.io/settings/profile). The action is immediate and cannot be reversed. It will:

- Erase all personal data tied to your account
- Cancel any active subscription
- Remove saved videos and cloud storage connections

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is permanent. There is no recovery option once confirmed. Download any content you want to keep before proceeding.
</Warning>
